# 📌 CRM Sales Performance & Account Analytics

## 📖 Overview
This project evaluates multi-dimensional sales performance and agent productivity using a Customer Relationship Management (CRM) dataset comprising **$10.01M in total closed revenue** across **7,000 total deals**. Moving beyond high-level numbers, the analysis examines sales velocity, individual representative efficiency, win/close rates, and key account concentration to equip leadership with actionable data for sales coaching and strategy.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
* **Python (Pandas & NumPy):** Data cleaning, preprocessing, handling missing values, transformation, and feature structuring.
* **Power BI:** Data modeling, DAX measure creation, interactive dashboarding, and visual reporting.

---

## 📉 Visualization Breakdown & Analytical Insights

### 1. Executive KPI Cards
* **Total Closed Amount ($10.01M):** Summarizes overall revenue generated across all closed deals, establishing the baseline financial scale of the dataset.
* **Total Deals (7K):** Represents the total volume of deals processed within the CRM.
* **Avg Weeks to Close (6.85 Weeks):** Tracks the average sales cycle length, measuring sales velocity and operational efficiency.
* **Potential to Close ($3.89M):** Highlights active pipeline revenue currently in progress, aiding in short-term sales forecasting.
* **Close Rate (51.06%):** Indicates overall conversion efficiency, showing that over half of all created opportunities are successfully won.

## 💡 Strategic Business Outcomes & Insights
* **Benchmarking High Performers:** Contrasted high-velocity reps (fast closers like Cecily Lampkin) against top revenue drivers (Darcel Schlecht) to set baseline targets for sales team training.
* **Key Account Optimization:** Highlighted high-value accounts—such as *Kan-code* and *Konex*, which led in both deal volume and total closed revenue—to prioritize account management resources.
* **Pipeline Actionability:** Identified $3.89M in potential closings to aid leadership in quarterly revenue forecasting and pipeline bottleneck resolution.
